# Spec: Disable Window Polish

## Duration
- Slider, 5–120 min in 5-min steps
- One color drives the number, track fill and thumb: white (0) → gold (30) → red (90) → blood black (120); the number trembles near the top

## Eye
- Starts as the gold closed mark with its stars, turns red while opening
- Both stars become small eyes at the same time as the main eye; every iris follows the cursor; all blink together
- The open-eye toolbar icon carries the star eyes too (16px hand-pixeled)

## Blocked text (YouTube)
- Cannot be selected or copied
