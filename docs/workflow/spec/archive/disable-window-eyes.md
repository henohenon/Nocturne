# Spec: Disable Window Eyes

## Eye
- Upper lid rises to reveal the iris (no squash); slight overshoot, then settles
- Stars: gold → black disc; a red dot inside opens with the same timing and motion as the lid; each blinks on its own period
- Every iris / red dot follows the cursor from its own position

## Dread zone (over 90 min)
- Number: color-split slices, flicker; at 120 a one-frame "見てるよ"
- Room: faster heartbeat, darkness closing in, scanlines; at the max the window jolts once, then shakes irregularly
- Eyes (main and stars): widen, pupils slit (from 60), stop blinking; they always keep following the cursor
- Caption escalates: 分だけ無効化 → 分も無効化 → 分も……？ → 分。本気？

## Streak confrontation
- Starts calm whatever the minutes; hovering or focusing "それでも解く" (locked or not) awakens it: full dread, the eyes keep following the cursor; leaving calms it

## Popup
- While the seal is broken the mark opens red and the wordmark turns crimson (same rule as the toolbar icon)
- Hovering or focusing "今すぐ再有効化" closes the eye back to the gold mark — a preview of the seal

## Toolbar icon
- Stars as black discs with red dots (32px / 16px: hand-placed red pixels)
