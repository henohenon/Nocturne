# Spec: Music Badge Allowlist

## Allowlist (YouTube)
- Visible: playlist / mix; or music that is not live
- Music = official artist channel label, **or** YouTube's music note on the thumbnail duration badge
- Live streams stay blocked, music or not

## Acceptance
- Covers / vocaloid marked with ♪ pass; non-music (games, vlogs) stays blocked; live stays blocked
- Music YouTube doesn't mark with ♪ stays blocked (accepted limit)
