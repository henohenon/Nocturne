# Business: Music Badge Allowlist

## Why
- Intentional listening is supposed to pass, but covers, vocaloid, utaite, and lofi were blocked: only official artist channels counted as music
- On the home "音楽" filter most items were blocked

## Goals
- Let through what YouTube itself classifies as music, still without judging content ourselves

## Non-goals
- Guessing music from titles or channel names
