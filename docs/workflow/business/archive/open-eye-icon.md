# Business: Open-Eye Toolbar Icon

## Why
- While the seal is broken, nothing in the browser reminds you; the toolbar icon should make the broken state impossible to ignore

## Goals
- Toolbar icon turns into a red open eye during a manual disable; back to the closed eye on expiry or re-enable
- Night mode (legitimate free time) keeps the closed eye

## Non-goals
- Touching YouTube / X pages (favicons, titles) — considered and dropped by the user
