# Business: X Timeline Seal

## Why
- X's endless feeds (home timeline, explore/trends) drain time the same way YouTube recommendations do
- Notifications, DMs, and posting are intentional uses and should stay free

## Goals
- Seal the concepts that swallow time: home timeline, explore, the trends / who-to-follow sidebar
- One checkpoint: the same disable, night schedule, and night seal as YouTube

## Non-goals
- Fine-grained per-post filtering, per-account rules, blocking other people's posts or profiles
