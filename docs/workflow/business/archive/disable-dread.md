# Business: Disable Dread

## Why
- The redesigned disable window looks elegant but not frightening; the moment of breaking the seal should feel like waking something
- The streak prompt is a native browser dialog — it looks like a system alert, is dismissed by reflex, and breaks the mood right when pressure matters most
- Gold-on-black blocked items on YouTube draw the eye; blocked content should recede, not decorate

## Goals
- Disable window: the closed eye opens and watches; the page feels alive and hostile
- Streak prompt: a custom, full-window confrontation that cannot be clicked through instantly, harder the more you repeat
- Blocked items: quiet gray

## Non-goals
- Changing streak rules, durations, or copy meaning
