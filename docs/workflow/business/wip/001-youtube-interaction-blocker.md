# Business Context: YouTube Interaction Blocker

## Why

### Problem
Users may want to control their YouTube browsing behavior by preventing accidental or impulsive clicks on video content. Current browsing lacks built-in mechanisms to enforce intentional viewing habits.

### Goal
Provide a Chrome extension that empowers users to maintain focus and intentionality when visiting YouTube by disabling video interaction capabilities.

### Value Proposition
- **Self-control**: Help users stick to their intended browsing behavior
- **Distraction reduction**: Prevent automatic video playback triggers
- **Mindful consumption**: Encourage deliberate content consumption choices

## Stakeholders
- **Primary**: Individual users seeking to manage their YouTube consumption
- **Secondary**: Productivity-focused users, parents managing children's browsing

## Success Criteria
- Users can visit YouTube without triggering unwanted video interactions
- Extension can be easily enabled/disabled as needed
- Minimal impact on page load performance
- Reliable operation across YouTube's UI updates

## Constraints
- Must work as Chrome extension (browser-based solution)
- Should not require server-side components
- Must respect YouTube's terms of service
- Should be maintainable with minimal ongoing updates
